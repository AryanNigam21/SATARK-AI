About SATARK AI

SATARK AI is a functional front-end prototype for investor scam resilience. It combines transparent safety rules with an explainable risk presentation. A production implementation can connect multilingual NLP/LLM analysis, verified public sources, URL reputation services and voice support.

Prototype safety: Never enter passwords, OTPs, PINs, bank credentials or other sensitive information.
